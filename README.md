# The Summer I Turned Pretty 🌸

## My Favorite Series

**The Summer I Turned Pretty** is my favorite series because it shows love, friendship, heartbreak, and the beauty of growing up. It reminds me of unforgettable summer memories and first love.

### Why I Love It
- The romantic storyline.
- The beautiful beach setting.
- The chemistry between the characters.
- The emotional and relatable moments.

### My Favorite Characters
1. Belly Conklin
2. Conrad Fisher
3. Jeremiah Fisher
4. Taylor Jewel
